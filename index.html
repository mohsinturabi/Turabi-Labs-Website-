FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I use Turabi Labs for just one restaurant location?

Yes. The Starter plan is built for a single location, with the option to add more locations as you grow into Business or Enterprise.

Do customers need to download an app to order?

No. Customers simply scan the QR code at their table and order directly from their browser — nothing to install.

How does payment settlement work?

Payments are processed through our integrated payment gateway and settle directly to your linked bank account, with every transaction visible in your dashboard.

When is the Doctor Appointment Booking system launching?

It's currently in development as part of our clinic product line, alongside Clinic Management, CRM, and WhatsApp Automation.

Can I migrate from my current POS system?

Yes, our onboarding team helps import your existing menu, pricing, and staff data so you can switch over without losing history.
